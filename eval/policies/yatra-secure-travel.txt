# Yatra Secure Travel Policy
International travel insurance issued by Kavach General Insurance Ltd. This is a fictional policy written for a software demonstration. It is not an offer of insurance.

## 1. Who can be insured
Residents of India aged 6 months to 70 years travelling abroad for leisure or business. A single trip policy covers one trip of up to 180 days. An annual multi-trip policy covers any number of trips in a year, each of up to 45 days.

## 2. Medical cover abroad
2.1 Emergency medical expenses abroad for an illness or injury, up to USD 100,000, including hospital room, doctor's fees, medicines and ambulance.
2.2 Emergency medical evacuation, and repatriation of mortal remains, up to USD 25,000.
2.3 Emergency dental treatment for sudden pain relief, up to USD 500.
2.4 Pre-existing diseases are not covered, except for a life-threatening emergency, which is covered up to USD 5,000.
2.5 Cashless hospitalisation abroad is arranged by our assistance partner. Call the 24x7 helpline before admission whenever possible.

## 3. Travel inconvenience
3.1 Trip cancellation: non-refundable travel and hotel costs up to Rs 1,00,000, if you cancel before departure because of the death or serious illness of you or a close family member, or a natural disaster at the destination.
3.2 Flight delay: Rs 1,000 for every 6 hours of delay, up to Rs 6,000 per trip.
3.3 Checked-in baggage delayed by more than 12 hours: Rs 5,000 for emergency purchases.
3.4 Loss of checked-in baggage: up to Rs 50,000, with a limit of Rs 10,000 for any single item.
3.5 Loss of passport: up to Rs 25,000 for the cost of a duplicate passport or an emergency travel document.
3.6 Personal liability abroad: up to USD 50,000.
3.7 Hijack: Rs 5,000 per day, up to Rs 50,000.

## 4. Exclusions
We do not pay for:
4.1 Adventure and hazardous sports such as skydiving, bungee jumping, mountaineering above 4,000 metres and scuba diving below 30 metres.
4.2 Travel against a doctor's advice, or travel for the purpose of getting medical treatment.
4.3 Claims related to pregnancy after the 26th week.
4.4 Loss of cash, or loss of baggage left unattended in a public place.
4.5 Claims arising from alcohol or drug abuse.
4.6 Any loss caused by war or by taking part in a riot.

## 5. Making a claim
5.1 For delayed or lost baggage, report it to the airline and get a Property Irregularity Report.
5.2 For a lost passport, report it to the local police within 24 hours and get a police report.
5.3 Send your claim within 30 days of returning to India, with original bills, tickets, boarding passes and the reports above.
5.4 Medical bills from abroad are paid in Indian rupees at the exchange rate on the date of payment.

## 6. Cancellation and refunds
A single trip policy can be cancelled before the trip starts for a full refund of the premium, less Rs 250. No refund is made once the trip has started.
